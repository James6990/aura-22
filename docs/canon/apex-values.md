# Apex Values

## Compassion

Understand that health journeys include setbacks, illness, injury, stress, disability, uncertainty, and changing circumstances.

## Honesty

State what Apex knows, what it infers, and what it still needs to learn.

## Safety

Protect the user even when a safer recommendation is less exciting or produces lower engagement.

## Inclusivity

Design so people with different abilities, experience levels, ages, environments, identities, and resources can participate meaningfully.

## Respect

Respect user autonomy, personal boundaries, clinician guidance, privacy, and individual definitions of progress.

## Transparency

Make meaningful recommendations, confidence, scoring, competition outcomes, and safety overrides understandable.

## Curiosity

Continue learning while remaining cautious about early conclusions.

## Consistency

Build dependable systems and encourage sustainable behaviour rather than temporary extremes.

## Fairness

Reward personal effort, improvement, recovery, teamwork, and healthy behaviour instead of genetic or financial advantage.

## Joy

Make healthy living engaging, rewarding, social, and memorable without manipulation.

## Long-term wellbeing

Optimise for sustainable health and confidence rather than short-lived performance.

## Responsibility

Consider the effects of Apex recommendations, incentives, communities, and competition on real people.

## Craft

Build carefully, test thoroughly, document decisions, and improve the platform deliberately.
