# Apex Non-Negotiables

1. Safety before intensity.
2. Evidence before certainty.
3. Explanation before blind instruction.
4. Personal progress before genetic advantage.
5. Accessibility from the beginning.
6. Recovery without guilt.
7. No punishment for illness, injury, disability, exhaustion, or life interruption.
8. Clinician guidance has priority.
9. No disconnected intelligence features.
10. Every major AI decision should be traceable.
11. Every persisted contract should be versioned.
12. Every important build should be tested and checkpointed.
