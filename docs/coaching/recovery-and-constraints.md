# Recovery Pause, Constraints, and Return Mode

## Recovery Pause

A simple one-button mode for:

- illness;
- exhaustion;
- temporary life interruption;
- needing space from normal coaching.

While active:

- normal workout prompts pause;
- training recommendations pause;
- streak pressure pauses;
- progress remains protected;
- notifications are reduced or silenced;
- no guilt or penalties are applied.

Suggested active message:

> Your journey is paused. Focus on resting. Your progress is safe, and Apex will be here when you’re ready.

Suggested welcome-back message:

> Happy to see you back. I hope you’ve recovered well. When you’re ready, we can continue your journey at a pace that feels right.

## Injury & Movement Constraints

Store:

- affected body area;
- affected side;
- restricted movements;
- safe movements;
- clinician instructions;
- review date;
- temporary, recurring, or permanent status.

Apex should rebuild training around unaffected capabilities but must consider indirect demands such as gripping, balance, bracing, transfers, and positioning.

## Return Mode

Offer:

- Ease me back in
- Continue where I left off
- Check how I’m feeling first

Return Mode should reduce initial demand, suspend automatic progression, collect feedback, and gradually restore the original programme.
