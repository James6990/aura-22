# Apex Bloodlines

## Definition

Bloodlines are long-term communities built around encouragement, shared progress, and healthy competition.

They are more meaningful than temporary teams or ordinary guilds.

## Bloodline contributions

Members may contribute through:

- workouts;
- nutrition;
- hydration;
- recovery;
- consistency;
- improvement;
- completing safe personal plans;
- supporting other members.

## Bloodline vs Bloodline

Bloodline vs Bloodline is a flagship seasonal competition.

It should:

- use adaptive scoring;
- allow all members to contribute;
- avoid dominance by elite athletes;
- reward balanced healthy behaviour;
- include transparent results;
- preserve safety and accessibility;
- prohibit pay-to-win advantages.
