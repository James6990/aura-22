# Apex Future Enhancements

## Purpose

This file captures valuable ideas that do not belong in the current milestone.

Recording an idea here does not automatically approve it for implementation.

Each idea must later be assessed for:

- user value;
- safety;
- evidence;
- accessibility;
- architectural fit;
- privacy;
- fairness;
- development cost;
- roadmap stage.

## Status labels

- **Build Now** — required by the active milestone.
- **Future Enhancement** — valuable but assigned to a later stage.
- **Research Required** — more evidence or exploration is needed.
- **Implemented** — completed and linked to a checkpoint.
- **Rejected** — not proceeding, with the reason recorded.
- **Superseded** — replaced by a better design.

# AI and Coaching

## Adaptive coaching communication style

**Status:** Future Enhancement  
**Target stage:** Advanced Coaching

Allow Apex to learn whether a user prefers:

- concise or detailed explanations;
- gentle or direct coaching;
- frequent or limited encouragement;
- visual or text-first guidance;
- proactive or user-requested suggestions.

The system must not manipulate emotions or reduce important safety information.

## Confidence timelines

**Status:** Future Enhancement  
**Target stage:** Event Analytics / Advanced Coaching

Show how Apex's confidence in progression, recovery, behaviour, and memory changes over time.

Users should be able to understand why confidence rose or fell.

## Coaching decision comparison

**Status:** Future Enhancement  
**Target stage:** Event Analytics

Allow internal analysis of:

- recommendation;
- evidence available at the time;
- user response;
- outcome;
- later learning;
- whether Apex would make the same decision now.

# Recovery Intelligence

## Pattern-based recovery prevention

**Status:** Future Enhancement  
**Target stage:** Recovery Intelligence Polish

Identify repeatable relationships such as:

- reduced sleep before demanding sessions;
- hydration patterns linked to readiness;
- certain schedules linked to missed workouts;
- repeated discomfort after specific movements.

Recommendations must distinguish correlation from established cause.

## Recovery confidence history

**Status:** Future Enhancement  
**Target stage:** Event Analytics

Show users how recovery recommendations became more personalised as evidence accumulated.

## Context-sensitive Recovery Pause

**Status:** Future Enhancement  
**Target stage:** Recovery, Safety, and Return-to-Training

Recovery Pause may eventually offer optional context such as:

- illness;
- exhaustion;
- caring responsibilities;
- travel disruption;
- emotional overload;
- clinician-requested rest.

The mode must remain simple and usable without requiring a reason.

# Journey Experience

## Journey stories

**Status:** Future Enhancement  
**Target stage:** Social, Community, and Journey Systems

Turn meaningful milestones into chapters that reflect:

- persistence;
- recovery;
- personal bests;
- returning after interruption;
- helping others;
- changes in confidence;
- long-term consistency.

Stories should celebrate the user's real journey rather than inventing achievements.

## Personal journey map

**Status:** Planned  
**Target stage:** Social, Community, and Journey Systems

Create a visual long-term path containing meaningful milestones, decisions, recovery periods, achievements, and community memories.

# Bloodlines and Community

## Mentor Bloodlines

**Status:** Future Enhancement  
**Target stage:** Bloodlines

Recognise experienced or supportive members for:

- welcoming newcomers;
- offering encouragement;
- sharing safe guidance;
- helping members remain consistent;
- supporting responsible recovery.

Mentorship reputation must not depend only on athletic performance.

## Positive contribution reputation

**Status:** Future Enhancement  
**Target stage:** Bloodlines / Social

Reward helpful, respectful, and encouraging community behaviour.

The design needs moderation, abuse prevention, and safeguards against popularity contests.

## Global cooperative events

**Status:** Future Enhancement  
**Target stage:** Seasonal Community Systems

Allow Bloodlines to contribute toward shared goals in addition to competing.

Possible contribution categories:

- movement;
- recovery;
- hydration;
- consistency;
- community encouragement;
- accessible participation.

# PvP and Gamification

**Status:** Planned  
**Target stage:** Fair PvP

Match or score users through relevant capability and behaviour signals rather than raw output alone.

## Recovery contribution scoring

**Status:** Planned

## Transparent scoring explanations

**Status:** Planned  
**Target stage:** Fair PvP

# Accessibility and Rehabilitation

## Accessibility preference learning

**Status:** Future Enhancement  
**Target stage:** Accessibility and Rehabilitation

Allow Apex to remember interaction formats and movement adaptations that improve usability, with clear user control.

## Assisted exercise pathways

**Status:** Planned  
**Target stage:** Accessibility and Rehabilitation

Support movements involving:

- assistance from another person;
- adaptive equipment;
- seated positions;
- supported balance;
- transfer considerations;
- restricted grip;
- limited range of motion.

## Accessibility-aware competition evidence

**Status:** Research Required  
**Target stage:** Fair PvP / Accessibility

Design anti-cheat and evidence systems that do not incorrectly penalise adaptive movement, assisted movement, alternative equipment, or disability-specific execution.

# Research

## Long-term autonomous learning safeguards

**Status:** Research Required  
**Target stage:** Autonomous Learning Engine

Before autonomous learning can affect live coaching, define:

- approval thresholds;
- contradiction handling;
- rollback;
- audit history;
- confidence decay;
- user correction;
- clinician overrides;
- harmful-pattern detection;
- learning retirement.

## Ethical engagement measurement

**Status:** Research Required  
**Target stage:** Product Analytics

Measure whether Apex improves sustainable health behaviour without optimising for compulsive use.

Potential success measures may include:

- safe consistency;
- return after interruption;
- reduced guilt;
- improved confidence;
- appropriate recovery;
- user understanding;
- long-term retention without pressure.
